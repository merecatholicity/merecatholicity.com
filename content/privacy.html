Privacy policy

This is the plain account of what we collect, what stays public, what stays private, and how long any of it lives. We ask for no email, no legal name, and no account. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your data to anyone. The rest is below.

Everything here is public, except your messages

The platform is a public square. Your posts and replies on the community boards, your profile, your wall, and your feed are public by design. Anyone may read them, and you should write as though the whole world were watching, because it can. The one private room is your direct messages.

Your direct messages

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. They are sealed inside your own browser before they ever reach us, and we hold nothing but the ciphertext. We cannot read them, and so we do not read them, and we could not sell what we cannot read even if we wished to. We guard them by reasonable means and by their very design. They are also set to expire, the way a message fades on a platform like Snapchat, so a conversation does not linger forever unless you choose to save it.

What we collect

Your identity is a key made in your own browser. We store only a one-way hash of that key and the pseudonym we derive from the hash, never the key itself. We record for a limited time the network addresses you connect from, so that we can keep out spam and abuse and enforce the bans we must. That is the whole of it. There is no tracking pixel here selling you to a broker.

How long we keep things

We do our best to keep what ought to be kept, but we promise nothing. Much of the platform is built on purpose to be ephemeral. Direct messages expire on their clock. Other things age out on theirs. Treat nothing here as permanent, and keep your own copy of anything you could not bear to lose.

What may change

We may make more of the platform ephemeral over time. We are weighing whether to let wall posts and the feed expire the way direct messages do, and to leave only the community boards standing. That is not yet decided. We reserve the right to make that change, and any and all others, to this policy and to the platform, at any time.
