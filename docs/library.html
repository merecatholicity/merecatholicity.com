The Second Temple, the world of the New Testament

The Judaism our Lord and His apostles lived in, heard in its own voice. Josephus is the outside witness to Christ, to John the Baptist, to James the brother of the Lord, and to the destruction of Jerusalem he watched with his own eyes. Philo of Alexandria carries the Logos vocabulary and the allegorical reading that the Fathers inherited. The Book of Enoch is quoted in the Epistle of Jude, and with Jubilees and the Testaments it opens the apocalyptic world the Gospels speak into. The Apocrypha themselves stand with the Scriptures above, in the Douay-Rheims Bible.
