# The write-path port: dissolving the classic client into the Lit shell (plan, 2026-09-17)

A hand-over note in the sense of CLAUDE.md's "a review or a hand-over note is a dated file
under `docs/architecture/reviews/`". **Status: P0's bundle half shipped 2026-09-17 (`d76b0e9`); P1's transport slice shipped
2026-09-18; the plan was re-sequenced the same day — P1 forward, P5 (merecat) and P6 (the DM thread),
a third of its days, behind the phases that have readers. Everything else is designed and not begun.** It is the phased design the
owner asked for on 2026-09-17, after the standing review's "two UIs" finding turned out to be
stale (the log's *One UI*, 2026-09-16, had already retired the thirteen classic twins). The
question the owner asked — "who uses the classic UI, can we drop it?" — has the answer below:
nobody uses it *instead of* Lit; every app page runs both halves; the classic half is the write
side, and it dissolves only by being ported, one phase at a time.

Lit layer cannot stand without the kit today, and the classic read layer no longer exists.

## 2. The goal

Every screen a Lit element under `app/views/`; every service an `app/` module; one bundle graph
(`docs/app.js` + lazy chunks under `docs/chunks/`); the shell's router the only boot.
`client/`, `mcBoot`, `mcKit` and `mcViews` gone. CODEBASE.md's own words: "the further step is
moving them into their Lit views until the classic render paths dissolve."

## 3. Invariants for every phase

Each carries its test in the same commit; a phase that cannot keep one does not ship.

1. **`docs/app.js` never rises above its ceiling** (`tests/js/bundle_budget`). Ported code lands
   in a **chunk**; a reader of a KJV chapter never downloads a write path.
2. **A law's sweep moves with its code, never deleted**: the Turnstile mount sweep
   (`tests/js/turnstile_warm.test.mjs`), one press-and-hold surface, DM privacy, media hygiene,
   the softnav `isConnected` guards, the keyboard shackle, the boot-signal rule.
3. **A screen ships only with its headless suite green on prod**; keyed screens use the CDP
   fixture-render stub in `webtest/live_kit.py` (synthetic rows, zero writes).
4. **Inside a phase, no behaviour change** beyond the deletion of the classic body it replaces.
   The revert of a phase is the classic body returning.
5. **`?app=0` keeps meaning "no chrome, no soft navigation, no SW"** — never "no views".
6. **Every element cleans up in `disconnectedCallback`**: listeners, timers, sockets scoped to
   the screen. This is the boot-signal law re-expressed for elements.
7. **The kit only shrinks.** No new `mcKit` member after P1; each phase records the count.

## 4. The phases (agent-days; each independently shippable)

**P0 — the frame (2). The bundle half is SHIPPED 2026-09-17** (commit `d76b0e9`): `app/shell.ts` builds as a split ESM
bundle and `docs/nav.js` injects it `type="module"`. No chunk yet (the shell has no dynamic
import), 24 bytes smaller, byte-deterministic; `tests/js/bundle_budget` locks the two halves —
the module injection and the single chunk wipe ahead of both builds. Verified against a local
`make serve`: shell ready, kernel and API on `window`, thirteen views registered, the classic
client still booting, the board index rendering, a soft hop still soft.

**The router half of P0 is not begun.** It is: a shell router keyed by the kernel's
`Domain.Route.parseRoute` (`purescript/src/Domain/Route.purs`, already the classic router's
decision) mounting `<mc-*>` into `main`, after which `boots()` (`app/shell.ts:619`) stops
handing `mcBoot` the routes the router owns and keeps handing it the rest. Its test: the route
table covers every `Route` constructor, exhaustively, as `tests/purescript/route.test.mjs`
does for the kernel.

**P1 — services out of the boot (3). The transport slice SHIPPED 2026-09-18** (`app/transport.ts`:
`fetchRetry`, `freshOpts`/`stampFresh`, `freshParam`, `cachedJson`, `peekJson` — moved verbatim over a
static `app/store.ts` import; the 27 view call sites import them; `app/api.ts` stopped being handed a
transport and the shell's 500 ms × 8 s wait for `window.mcKit` is deleted; the classic client keeps its
six names as one-line doors to `window.mcTransport`. **Kit 71 → 65.** The two laws the move creates —
one store, never capture `fetch` — are swept by `tests/js/transport.test.mjs`.) The identity, Turnstile,
surface, admin-core, dm-crypto and dm-styles slices are not begun.
Nothing renders differently; the kit shrinks from 66 to
about 30. Measured 2026-09-17, the Lit layer reaches **66 distinct kit members**; by use:
`state` 44 · `API` 30 · `isAdmin` 14 · `displayName` 13 · `freshParam` 12 · `freshOpts` 12 ·
`fmtDateTime` 10 · `fetchRetry` 10 · `cachedJson` 9 · `el` 7 · `blockedOut` 7 ·
`fmtTimeCompact` 6 · `peekJson` 5 · `commentNode` 5, then a long tail of ones and twos. The
first slice is therefore the transport group (`fetchRetry`, `cachedJson`, `peekJson`,
`freshParam`, `freshOpts` — 48 call sites), which also frees `app/api.ts` from being handed its
transport by comments.js at wire-up; the classic client then delegates to it the way it already
delegates to `window.mcCore` and `window.mcRich`. `state` and `API` are the two the port cannot
take until identity moves (they are the boot's own), so they go with the identity slice.
- `fetchRetry`, `cachedJson`, `peekJson`, `freshParam`/`freshOpts` → `app/transport.ts`, so
  `app/api.ts` stops receiving its transport from comments.js at wire-up.
  (Since 2026-09-17 the shell wraps `fetch` itself — `app/wirecheck.ts`, the `Domain.Wire` list
  check — and `app/store.ts` refuses a malformed answer; `app/transport.ts` inherits both by calling
  `fetch` and the store, and must not capture a `fetch` reference before the shell installs.)
- Identity (`mintIdentity`, `loginWithKey`, `clearKey`, `state.key`/`myHash`, the "established"
  reading) → `app/identity.ts` over `app/store.ts`; `Domain.Auth` stays the rule.

  **Measured 2026-09-18, and it reorders the rest of P1: identity is not one slice among six, it
  is the gate.** Every remaining module funnels through the boot's `state` (and `bootSig`). Counting
  the names each `install<Feature>(B)` binds, and how many of them are those two:

  | module | binds | of which state/bootSig | module | binds | of which |
  |---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
  | `dm-thread` | 70 | 2 | `merecat` | 34 | 2 |
  | `board` | 64 | 1 | `dm-message` | 28 | 2 |
  | `profile` | 58 | 2 | `composer` | 26 | 2 |
  | `wall` | 52 | 1 | `admin` | 21 | 1 |
  | `admin-core` | 16 | 1 | `dm-inbox` | 19 | 2 |
  | `dm-pickers` | 18 | 1 | `surface` | 11 | 2 |
  | `dm-styles` | 3 | 1 (vestigial — the body uses only `el`) | | | |

  Thirteen of thirteen. So the P1 bullets below identity cannot be taken in any order: `surface.ts`
  reads `state` and `bootSig` and cannot move to `app/` before they do, and the same is true of
  `admin-core.ts`. The plan already said `state` and `API` "go with the identity slice"; what the
  count adds is that this is not a convenience — **identity is the only P1 slice that unblocks
  another, and until it ships the remaining five are blocked, not merely unscheduled.**

  Two consequences for whoever takes it:

  1. **It collides with the standing review's P0.** The platform review's security ladder
     (`docs/architecture/reviews/2026-09-17-platform-review.md`) has its own identity work in
     flight — the weak-key floor, and layer 3's `profiles.pubid` decoupling the published id from
     `SHA-256(key)`. Both touch `client/profile.ts`, `Domain.Auth` and `app/core.ts`, which is
     exactly where `app/identity.ts` would land. **Sequence them, do not parallelise them**: the
     security ladder first (it is live-exploitable and its layer 3 changes what an identity *is*),
     the port's identity slice onto the settled shape afterwards.
  2. **`dm-styles.ts` is the one exception and can be taken any time** — 240 lines that are a CSS
     string and an injection, using only `el`; `section` and `state` are bound and never read,
     vestigial from the Wave F split. Moving it to `styles/` also satisfies CLAUDE.md's "a
     JS-injected style block must agree with the stylesheet (it is injected later and wins)" by
     construction rather than by vigilance — but it must be APPENDED to the cascade, since winning
     over `main.css` is what the block does today, and it needs DM screens verified with the
     fixture-render stub before it ships.
- Turnstile (`renderTurnstileWidget`, `loadTurnstile`, `client/comments.ts:885`) →
  `app/turnstile.ts`; the sweep's law becomes "only `app/turnstile.ts` mounts, only from the
  focus net or a press, never for a spared identity" — re-pointed, not deleted.
- `client/surface.ts` → `app/surface.ts` near-verbatim (`openActs`, `armHold`, `postMenu`, the
  reactions ledger); `client/admin-core.ts` → `app/admin-core.ts`; `client/dm-crypto.ts` →
  `app/dm/crypto.ts` (nacl, E1, E3, safety numbers — untouched logic).
- `client/dm-styles.ts` → `styles/` CSS; the injected block retires (the "must agree with the
  stylesheet" law is satisfied by having one copy).
- The two frozen copies behind `if (window.mcRich)` / `if (window.mcCore)` in the classic
  helpers (`appendRich`, `isMember`, `profileLimits`) become unconditional calls; their bodies go.

**P2 — the admin consoles (2).** `viewAdmins`, `viewShadowbans`, `viewIpBans`, `viewAudit`,
`viewDiscordHooks`, `viewPlatformSettings`, `renderPending` (`client/admin.ts`, ~1,050 lines) →

for the write-path headless checks (today's `webtest/.testkeys` is a read-only probe) — or the
owner's manual pass on each console after the deploy, recorded in the log.

**P3 — board writes, feed, journal, saved, join, recent, post (4).** `<mc-composer>` first: a

move once. Then `viewFeed` (`client/wall.ts`), `viewJournalArticle`, `viewSaved`, `viewJoin`,
`viewRecent`, `viewPost` → elements in a `board` chunk.

**P4 — the profile editors (2).** `renderProfile`, `renderIdentity`, `renderProfilePosts`,
`adminProfileEditor`, the avatar editor → inside `<mc-profile>` (which calls
`kit.renderProfile` today, `app/views/profile.ts:63`).

**P5 — merecat (3).** `viewMerecat` (1,422), `renderChats`, `renderQuota`,

**P6 — the DM thread (5).** `viewDm` (1,115), `client/dm-message.ts` (856),
`client/dm-pickers.ts` (284), the inbox's frame handlers (already shadowed by `app/live.ts` +

**P7 — dissolve (2).** Delete `client/`, `mcBoot`, `mcKit`, `mcViews`, the `client_modules`
law and `tests/_support/client.mjs`; `comments.js` leaves the eight app pages (`docs/nav.js`,

follow); CODEBASE.md, CLAUDE.md and README rewritten; the client any-baseline collapses with the
1,690 `var`s; the bundle budget re-based to the new graph.

Estimate ≈ 23 agent-days → five to six calendar weeks at one phase per push cycle, each phase
reviewed on prod before the next begins.

## 6. Decisions the owner makes along the way

- **P2:** an admin identity key on the dev box (a `.testkeys` entry) so the consoles can be
  verified headlessly — or the manual pass.
- **P3:** keep the composer wrapped as a DOM builder inside `<mc-composer>` (this plan's
  default: the laws move once) or rewrite it as templates in the same phase.
- **P0:** whether `?app=0` survives at all once the shell is the only boot (this plan keeps it).

## 7. Not in this plan

The worker is untouched. The PureScript kernel gains nothing but the route table's exhaustive
use. The board's read screens, the chrome, the dock and the calls engine are already where
they will stay.
